Our Services

Comprehensive paediatric and neonatal care for children from birth through adolescence, at Evercare Hospital Dhaka

Outpatient Clinic Services

🫁

Respiratory Illnesses

Diagnosis and management of Pneumonia, Asthma, bronchitis, recurrent respiratory infections and chronic lung conditions in children of all ages.

👂

Ear, Nose & Throat

Assessment and management of Tonsil and Adenoid problems, recurrent ear infections, hearing concerns and ENT-related breathing difficulties.

🫃

Gastrointestinal Disorders

Acute and chronic digestive problems including gastroenteritis, constipation, abdominal pain, feeding difficulties and inflammatory bowel conditions.

🦠

Infections & Allergies

Management of childhood infections, fever assessments, food allergies, allergic rhinitis, eczema-related allergies and immune responses.

🦴

Bone & Joint Problems

Evaluation of limping, joint pain, swelling and musculoskeletal complaints in children including inflammatory and infectious conditions.

🌱

Growth & Nutrition

Comprehensive assessment and management of growth faltering, malnutrition, obesity, nutritional deficiencies and dietary counselling for children.

🧴

Common Skin Conditions

Diagnosis and treatment of eczema, urticaria, childhood rashes, fungal infections and other common dermatological conditions in children.

👶

Newborn Care

Comprehensive assessment and management of newborn diseases both in outpatient clinic and inpatient settings. Expert neonatal consultations.

Inpatient & Intensive Care

🏥

Paediatric ICU Care

Excellent intensive care unit management for critically ill children. Close monitoring and expert treatment for severe and complex paediatric conditions requiring inpatient admission.

🔬

Investigations

All sorts of paediatric investigations are available at Evercare Hospital Dhaka — blood tests, imaging, microbiological studies, lung function tests and specialist diagnostics.
