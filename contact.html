How to Book an Appointment

1
Call the Evercare Call Centre

Dial 10678 (Dhaka). Lines are open Saturday to Thursday during clinic hours.

2
Request Dr M Quamrul Hassan

Ask specifically for an appointment with Dr M Quamrul Hassan, Senior Consultant Paediatrics and Neonatology.

3
Choose your preferred session

Morning (9am–1pm), Afternoon (2pm–4:30pm) or Evening (5pm–7:30pm). The call centre will confirm availability.

4
Alternatively — book telemedicine online

For online consultations visit ecare.evercarebd.com and book directly through the platform.
